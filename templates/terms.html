Terms of Use

These are the basic terms for using Matchday Brain.

Independent fan game

Matchday Brain is an independent football prediction game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to FIFA or any official tournament organiser.

Predictions

Predictions must be submitted before the match lock deadline shown on each fixture. Late entries are blocked by the app.

Results and scoring

Results are entered manually in the admin area. Leaderboards are based on saved predictions and the scoring rules shown in the app.

Fair use

Do not spam entries, impersonate other people, submit offensive names or attempt to manipulate the game.

Prizes

If prizes are added later, prize rules will be shown clearly before entry.
