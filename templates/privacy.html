Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains what Matchday Brain stores when you enter a prediction.

What we collect

When you submit a prediction, we may store your name, optional X handle, optional email address, country you are backing, predicted score, first-goal minute, fixture and submission time.

X handles

If you add an X handle, you are allowing Matchday Brain to include that handle in manual prediction, leaderboard and result posts. You can leave the X handle blank.

Emails

Email is optional and is intended for future prize, leaderboard or game updates. Do not enter an email address if you do not want us to store it.

Local saved details

The app may save your name, X handle and country in your browser so you do not need to retype them on the same device.

Contact

For now, contact Matchday Brain through the official X account or site owner.
